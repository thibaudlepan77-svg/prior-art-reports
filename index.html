Prior art reports on patents asserted in bulk

When one company files dozens of suits on the same patent, every defendant needs the same search. These reports are written once and sold to all of them at one price.

  • Torus Ventures and US 7,203,844, prior art for defendants. Two references from 1998 and 1999, not cited on the patent, charted against claims 1, 19 and 37 of US 7,203,844.
  • WebSock Global Strategies and US 7,756,983, prior art for defendants. An uncited 1999 HTTP patent and a 1999 IETF document, charted against claim 21 of US 7,756,983.
  • LearnMaster, US 12,572,265 and US 10,705,694, prior art for defendants. Two public descriptions of synchronized video watching from 2007 and 2008, not cited on either LearnMaster patent.

Each report charts the claims the plaintiff actually asserts, quotes every source word for word, lists the art of record so the references can be checked as new, and states its weak points.
