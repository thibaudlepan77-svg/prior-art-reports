LearnMaster, US 12,572,265 and US 10,705,694, prior art for defendants

Two public descriptions of synchronized video watching from 2007 and 2008, not cited on either LearnMaster patent.

The patent

US 12,572,265 B2 and US 10,705,694 B2, one family on synchronized presentation sessions. Earliest priority date 15 June 2010.

LearnMaster LLC sued Louper.io in March 2026, then Livestorm, Bending Spoons, Enghouse, Redbrick and Sony on 2 September 2026 in the Eastern District of Texas. The newer patent issued in March 2026. The art of record of both patents is mostly meeting and webinar software.

What the search found

  • A paper presented at a 2008 ACM conference by the research lab of a large web company. A messenger plug-in, a web player and a share button pull a friend into the video you are watching, in sync.
  • A consumer website from 2007 that invited friends to join a video session.

Where it is weak. Neither source describes an acceptance message sent back to the inviting computer, or a request that ends the session.
