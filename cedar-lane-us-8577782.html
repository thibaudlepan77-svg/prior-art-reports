Cedar Lane Technologies and US 8,577,782, prior art for defendants

Two uncited US applications from 2005 and 2007 that price quotes by each client's trading history and profitability, charted against claim 20 of US 8,577,782.

The patent

US 8,577,782 B2, "trading with conditional offers for semi-anonymous participants". Earliest priority date 8 April 2010, the filing date, with no earlier priority claim.

Cedar Lane Technologies Inc. has been suing brokers, banks and trading firms on this patent since January 2025, in Texas, New York, Florida, Illinois and Delaware. The complaints chart claim 20. The patent cites eighteen US documents and no non-patent literature, and I found no IPR or reexamination against it.

What the search found

  • A published application from 2007 describing an anonymous trading system that keeps each trader's history, measures how the price moved against the dealer after that trader's fills, sorts traders into risk classes and has dealers quote a separate two-sided price for each class.
  • A published application from 2005 by a large US bank describing a dealer quote service that prices each client's request with a constant tracking the historical profitability of trades with that client.

Where it is weak. Both references trade outside a registered exchange, and claim 20 says "exchange". The fee claims 22 to 27 and claim 32 are not covered. 35 U.S.C. 101 is outside the report.
